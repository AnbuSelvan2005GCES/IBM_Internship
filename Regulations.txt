CATEGORY: Regulations

Attendance Requirement:
Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each course per
semester to be eligible to sit for the end-semester examination. Students
falling between 65% and 75% attendance may apply for condonation through
the Head of Department, subject to valid supporting documents (medical
certificates, official participation letters, etc.). Students below 65%
attendance are detained and must repeat the course in a subsequent
semester.

Examination Eligibility:
A student must clear all internal assessments and maintain the required
attendance to be eligible for end-semester exams. Students who miss an
internal assessment due to a valid, documented reason may request a
make-up assessment within 10 working days of returning to college.

Re-evaluation and Revaluation:
Students may apply for revaluation of an answer script within 15 days of
result publication by paying the prescribed fee at the exam cell. Requests
after this window will not be entertained.

Grading System:
The college follows a 10-point relative grading system. Grade points range
from O (Outstanding, 10 points) down to F (Fail, 0 points). A minimum
cumulative CGPA of 5.0 is required for graduation.

Malpractice Policy:
Any student found using unauthorized material or engaging in impersonation
during an examination will have their result for that course cancelled and
may face suspension for one semester, as decided by the Discipline
Committee.
