PRINTER TROUBLESHOOTING

Problem:
Printer is not printing.

Steps:

1. Check whether the printer is powered on.
2. Check the USB or network connection.
3. Check whether the correct printer is selected.
4. Check the printer queue.
5. Cancel stuck print jobs.
6. Restart the printer.
7. Restart the computer.
8. Check whether the printer reports paper or ink problems.
9. If the printer is connected to a company network, contact IT support if the issue continues.
