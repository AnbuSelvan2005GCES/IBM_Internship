PASSWORD RESET

Problem:
User forgot their computer or application password.

Steps:

1. Verify the identity of the user according to company policy.
2. Use the application's official password-reset option.
3. Check whether a password-reset email was received.
4. Create a new strong password.
5. Do not share passwords with other users.
6. If the account is locked, contact the IT administrator.

Never ask the user to provide their existing password.
